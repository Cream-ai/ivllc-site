Front Office

Extra hands for your shop, without hiring or learning new software.

Joel works with you to build custom automations around how your business already runs. Think of it like a team of hired help that works for you, or backs up the employees you already have. Check in from your phone on the go or at your desk. Nothing ever goes out in your name without your yes.

You do not have to learn the tech. Joel stays on to run it.

You have enough on your hands without becoming an IT expert. We start with visible, low-risk work first so you can see the help working before we ever touch deeper parts of your business. When it is live, Joel does not disappear. The monthly relationship is the service: he keeps everything running, adjusts it as your shop changes, and stays a text or call away. He keeps you on the cutting edge, so you never have to worry about falling behind.

Examples of where we can start (examples only, not the full menu)

  • Missed-call rescue. When you are on a job and cannot pick up, the system drafts a polite text back to the caller, holds an open spot on your calendar, and leaves a clean note for you to review.
  • Invoice follow-up. Unpaid invoices stop sitting for weeks. Polite reminders are drafted in your shop's tone, friendly first and firmer only if needed, and nothing sends until you approve it.
  • Morning brief. Start the day with one plain page showing what came in overnight, who needs a callback, and which decisions are waiting on you.
  • Review and appointment replies. Drafted thank-you replies to customer reviews and timely appointment reminders wait for your quick yes.

These are examples only, and we tailor the work to whatever eats up your week. Nothing goes out in your name without your approval. Single owner-operators are $100 a month. Teams and larger operations: from $300/mo, quoted after an assessment.
