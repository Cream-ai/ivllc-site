Privacy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Interregnum Ventures LLC uses the information you give us only to do the work you hired us for — running your Front Office, handling Back Office work, or setting up a private on-premise system.

Nothing goes out in your name without your approval. Your accounts stay in your name.

We will never sell your data.

How we work with your information

We collect what we need to understand your week, set up the work, and stay in touch: things like your name, your shop, your email and phone number, and the files or access you choose to share.

We will not ask you to send passwords by email or text. When a connection to one of your tools is needed, we set it up with you present.

If you use our Private AI option, that work is set up so sensitive information stays on machines in your building, with the boundaries agreed in writing.
